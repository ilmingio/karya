# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work. Welcome newcomers. Give feedback that someone can act on.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, sexual language or imagery, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable. This applies to issues, pull requests, chat, and events related to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block participants who break this code. Reports can be made privately to the maintainers. Maintainers will respond with what they decided and, when it is safe, why.

Maintainers who break this code may lose maintainer status.
