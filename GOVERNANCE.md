# Governance

Karya is early. Until there are additional maintainers, the project maintainer decides what merges, what ships, and how security reports are handled.

## How decisions are made

- Small implementation choices can be made in a pull request.
- Choices that are hard to reverse are written down in `docs/adr/` before or alongside the change.
- The public roadmap in `ROADMAP.md` is the priority list. It can change when usage says it should.

## Maintainers

Maintainers review contributions, merge code, and cut releases. New maintainers are people who have already contributed and want to keep doing so.

## Separated products

`karya-vscode` and `karya-studio` will have their own repositories if they are created. They do not change the maintainer rules of this agent repository by themselves.
